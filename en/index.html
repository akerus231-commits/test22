What is Revanite?

Revanite is a strategic growth studio founded by Ruslan Khasanov. It has two service lines: Revanite Tracking — tracking and advisory for startups and business owners, and Revanite Studio — done-for-you marketing covering strategy, advertising and analytics. The studio's principle: marketing is an investment, not an expense.

Who does Ruslan Khasanov work with?

Small and mid-sized businesses in Kazakhstan and the CIS, early-stage startups and accelerator teams. Industry experience includes banking and fintech, insurance, education, B2B manufacturing, real estate, e-commerce and events. Clients include BI GROUP, Yeko and Jintropine.

How is a startup tracker different from a marketing contractor?

A tracker does not run the ads for the team. A tracker helps find the growth bottleneck, frame hypotheses, test them quickly and cheaply, and keep the team focused on metrics. A contractor executes the chosen solution. Ruslan works in both roles: tracking through Revanite Tracking and tech hubs, execution through Revanite Studio.

Where does Ruslan Khasanov teach?

At Almaty Management University (AlmaU) — web analytics, digital marketing, SMM and online marketing tools; at Qamalladin University, where he leads the Digital program; and at ICB college. He runs workshops at Astana Hub and has given a guest lecture at Nazarbayev University.

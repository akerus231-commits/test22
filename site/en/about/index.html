Ruslan Khasanov: biography

Growth strategist · Startup tracker · Digital marketing lecturer

Ruslan Khasanov is a marketing specialist and growth strategist based in Almaty, Kazakhstan. He came up through corporate marketing in financial services: he led small business marketing at Alfa Bank, digital marketing at Home Credit Bank and product at Halyk Insurance, and was part of the team that launched Samsung Kazakhstan's online store.

Today his work spans four connected lines. Tracking: he helps startups at Astana Hub, Almaty Hub, Terricon Valley, NURIS and IT Park find growth points, test hypotheses and land first customers. Execution: through his studio Revanite he runs advertising, analytics and strategy for small and mid-sized businesses across Kazakhstan and the CIS. Teaching: he lectures in digital marketing at AlmaU, Qamalladin University and ICB college. In-house: he heads digital marketing at the fiscal technology platform re:Kassa.

What ties it together is a tracker's approach: first find where growth is actually being lost, then decide what to do. Hence Revanite's principle — marketing as an investment, not an expense. Every channel has to pay back and be measurable, from click to paid deal in the CRM.
